## Full narration (plain text)

Welcome to HECP LOTO. Lockout / Tagout — document energy isolation procedures, generate QR tags, and track live lock state.

In this short tutorial, we'll walk through how to use the app.

First, register your organization. The first person to sign up becomes the administrator.

Enter your details and a password, then create the organization.

LOTO dashboard. Procedures, lock state and pending approvals at a glance.

Create a procedure. Add isolation points — energy source auto-numbers (E-1, M-1…), with hazard, photo and verification.

Procedure inventory. All procedures, filterable by site, equipment and status; regenerate QR/PDF tags.

LOTO operations. Technicians lock and unlock each isolation point; status flips to “Locked” when all points are secured.

Approvals. Safety / Engineering review and approve procedures before use.

Lock inventory. Track padlocks, hasps and chains across the organization.

HECP LOTO — isolate safely, every time.. Start by registering your organization.

That's a quick tour of HECP LOTO. Thanks for watching — get started by registering your organization.
