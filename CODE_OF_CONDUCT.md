# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward others.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility and apologizing for mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting/derogatory comments,
publishing others' private information, and other conduct that could reasonably
be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainers at **[conduct@your-company-domain]**. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban any
contributor for behaviors they deem inappropriate.
