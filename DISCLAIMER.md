## Regulatory compliance is the user's responsibility

Operators must independently ensure that every procedure and lockout complies
with all applicable regulations and standards, including but not limited to
**OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147 (The Control of Hazardous Energy)**, NFPA 70E, and any

develop, approve, apply, or remove energy-isolation devices.

## No verification of physical state

The application records the lock/unlock state that users enter. It **cannot
verify** that energy has actually been isolated, dissipated, or verified at the
equipment. Always perform a physical try-out / zero-energy verification before
work begins.
